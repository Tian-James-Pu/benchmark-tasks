# Finish cooking and serve one batch of gnocchi

Work in progress. Full-scene native qualification is incomplete. A resting-contact check and a deliberately unscored short manipulation diagnostic have run. Do not submit this folder or infer full feasibility from its CPU checks. No task.json or evaluation media has been uploaded.

The task is one roughly 110 g portion containing 32 fresh potato gnocchi, already partially cooked in one batch at reset. It requires actual float observation, one more minute in hot water, slotted-spoon retrieval and plating. There are no staged pairs or per-piece spoilage deadlines. The source recipe is [King Arthur's gnocchi class](https://shop.kingarthurbaking.com/content/class-pdfs/Gnocchi_June2024.pdf), page2. The full recipe yields4–8servings and calls for a4+quart pot; this task cooks one small portion in a smaller pan, not the complete recipe in miniature equipment.

Objects and starting layout use the YAM station world frame, metres, with the standard tabletop height `TABLE_TOP['station']`. The code fixes the layout and seed2048; object jitter is zero.

Only the installed hob is fixed. The pan, plate, spoon and every food piece are free native rigid bodies. There are no scripted object poses, reattachments, attraction fields or hidden restraints. The demo moves only the ordinary robot joints through native IK and action guards. Each food body receives Archimedes buoyancy at the displaced-volume centroid plus velocity-dependent water drag; the loose pan receives the opposite reaction. A flat liquid-surface approximation follows actual vessel pose, accounts for overflow and updates a nonphysical visual. Material loss updates pan mass and approximately scales inertia; water slosh, splash trajectories and the small centre-of-mass redistribution are omitted.

The raw/cooked density proxy is1080→950kg/m³ over2s, with fixed collision geometry and mass. It is an aggregate food/retained-gas flotation proxy through effective displaced volume, not a measured change in the density of solid dough. An [INRA experimental protocol](https://icmpg.hub.inrae.fr/content/download/314/2403?version=1), using flour-and-egg dough rather than this exact potato recipe, distinguishes starch hydration from vapour-assisted flotation and shows that flotation alone need not mean cooked through. This model omits explicit bubble attachment/loss and its dependence on squeezing, reimmersion and cooling. Water density1000kg/m³ differs from actual near-boiling water by about4%; the food density curve, drag and cooking ages are unmeasured estimates. Heat capacity4180J/(kg·K), stainless500J/(kg·K), latent heat2.26MJ/kg,75% heat-transfer efficiency and2W/K whole-vessel heat loss are declared engineering approximations. Front/rear power follows actual dial angle and pan/plate area overlap; only water at least95°C advances the cooking clock. Residual hot water remains valid afterOFF. These operational recipe checks are not a calibrated gelatinization or food-safety model. CPU sensitivity at twice the estimated heat loss maintains hot water with the declared setting; this is a calculation, not a hardware test.

Each piece earns1/32 when all of these hold: it has visibly floated unassisted, then accumulated60s at least75% immersed in water at least95°C; it has subsequently been supported out of water by the spoon bowl or a food stack rooted on it; it is now supported by the plate or a food stack rooted on it, with linear speed below10mm/s and angular speed below0.2rad/s continuously1s, with no spoon or robot support. Reimmersion requires later spoon retrieval but preserves cooking already completed. Early, dropped or unserved pieces earn no credit for themselves and do not erase the others' work. A piece subsequently carried by a gripper must be retrieved with the spoon again; it keeps its completed cooking. Incidental contact while actually supported by the plate/spoon does not reset eligibility. No generic permanent touch penalty is used.

Total score is capped at0.5 until both burners are within5° ofOFF, the spoon rests on the table and is released, both loose cookware items rest upright within3°, their speeds are below10mm/s and0.1rad/s, and no robot contact supports any task body, continuously1s. Full1.0 requires all 32pieces and these finishing conditions. The implementation fails closed on unresolved native contact actors or contact-buffer overflow. Actual positive upward normal contacts establish support; opposing normals cannot cancel into a false floating observation. Food stacks propagate plate, spoon and robot support. A separate native resting-food diagnostic verifies the measured opposite reaction on the plate:0.03355N upward for a3.420g piece. Signed native scalar forces are multiplied by their raw normal to obtain the force acting on the reporting body; magnitudes and acting normals are then used to reconstruct both actors and deduplicate matching reports. Zero-force contacts cannot supply support. The older positive-only diagnostic demonstrated the resting weight but does not establish that raw native reporting was one-sided. The corrected signed-force adapter has CPU regression evidence and still requires native qualification. Full manipulation contact coverage still requires qualification.

Real trial scoring uses continuous video, a stopwatch, a water thermometer, count of32 pieces, and the visible final plate/spoon/dials. For the ordinary whole-batch path, observing the last piece float and all remain in hot water one further minute is sufficient. For atypical partial/early-returned spoonfuls, review the per-piece or spoonful-cohort video timing; do not turn the whole-batch shortcut into a requirement that all 32stay present. Returned pieces resume their own hot-immersion clocks. Record imperfect support/temperature visibility as unadjudicable instead of guessing. The real check is a proposed protocol, not an already performed hardware validation.

Reset consumes food. Prepare fresh dough using the source recipe, take one weighed~110g portion, shape32consistent pieces, restore the stated water depth and hot starting condition, and introduce all pieces together. Refill/reset water and use new uncooked food after each trial; cooking cannot be reset by returning served food to the pan. The simulation starts with all pieces partially cooked,80s nominal immersion, still on the bottom. A real reproduction should record the actual starting immersion age and initial float state; the simulated density/time curve has not been calibrated to a specific flour/potato batch.

CPU checks: `python cpu_checks.py` verifies 20 physical/contact-adapter invariants; `cpu-ik.json` records the complete two-arm route under native limits, worst position error0.094mm. `python -m unittest test_recipe_scoring test_contact_adapter` runs the included outcome and contact-adapter adversarial tests. These do not replace native trials. The current scripted route is373s(7460 control steps) at20Hz, with ten available ordinary spoonfuls. At each scoop, demo-only native-state feedback selects a strip containing remaining immersed food and follows actual pan/plate translation. The robot still picks up and transfers food through native contacts. The revised route separates level lowering, tipping and lifting to avoid striking the plate. The dial policy uses short side-pinch turns with ordinary opening/regrasping in the front-facing clearance sector; it is under native qualification. A loaded diagnostic spoonful slipped during tipping at the first closure setting; a4mm deeper grasp, stronger ordinary joint closure and slower tipping subsequently transferred12pieces in a two-spoonful diagnostic and achieved a released spoon finish with unchanged native hardware limits and friction. That shortened diagnostic deliberately did not wait for all cooking criteria and is not a full-score demonstration. No model evaluation has run on this full package.

Mechanism helper `mechkit.py` derives from heat-shrink-lug, declaredMIT at upstream commit 83024cf323fe5abf8df2a00597b4d30bebfefd3d. Its local contact offset is 0.2 mm to resolve real spoon gaps and food contact. Sensed loose bodies have sleep_threshold=0 so resting physical contacts remain reportable. A local before-reset hook detaches the fluid callback during environment reset/settling, and filled mass/inertia are restored for the new episode. Passive dial joint configuration is restored only on genuine episode reset, never during trial dynamics. Scene, buoyancy, heat, contacts, scoring and policy adapter are independently authored.
