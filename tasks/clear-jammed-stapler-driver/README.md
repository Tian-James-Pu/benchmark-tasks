This first gate checks a native gripper grasp of the NVIDIA SimReady 4 mm slotted screwdriver and entry into a 6.8 mm service opening, with a physical slider opposed by a 2 N test load. It awards zero throughout. The load is a feasibility test, not measured P361 residual pressure. The battery, loading slide, actual bent obstruction and final safety scorer are not implemented yet.

The 274 x 224 x 81 mm housing envelope is an estimated P361-scale approximation, resting on its side. Internal geometry is bounded by the 6.35 mm narrow crown staple and the manufacturer's Fig11 service drawing; it is not a dimensioned reproduction. Exact source evidence is retained during development.
