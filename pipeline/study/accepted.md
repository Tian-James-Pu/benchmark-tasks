## Acceptance trend
Accepted per 250 PRs: 46 (0-249), ~16-23 (250-1749), 64 (1750-1999), 35, 36 (2000-2499), 8/250 so far for 2500+.
Old accepts (PR 6 Face cans, 31 Corner stop, 39 one bottle from row) were very simple; that bar is gone.
Category mix of the 60 most recent accepts: Societal relatability 26, Jobs 13, Safety 8, Other 6, AI timelines 3,
"Societal relevance" 4 (an invalid label, still accepted). Older 240: Jobs 131. So the shift is toward everyday tasks.

## Common structure of accepted tasks
1. One named real product / real-size object (brand+model, mm, g), often with a price or part number. Real-trial
   recipe: what to buy, tape marks, reset in ~2 min, gripper-pad protection, no live power/flame/sharp blade.
2. A single non-obvious physical knack stated in context in one sentence: "X does not stay put / springs back /
   hides its state, so one hand must hold A while the other does B", plus what goes wrong naively.
3. Claim with citation: SocRel = "most adults have done it" + maker's instructions quoted; Jobs = O*NET/SOC code,
   quoted task statement, worker count (>~500k), and frequency ("several a day"); Safety = regulator/standard
   (OSHA, FDA CPG, CDC/AAP, HSE, maker manual) and an UNSTATED hazard visible in the scene.
4. Scoring: 3-5 discrete levels (0/0.25/0.5/0.75/1.0 or additive), each a numeric condition checkable with ruler,
   protractor, feeler gauge, scale, photo, video; latched 0 gates for damage/hazard/forcing; 1.0 requires hands off
   (3-8 cm) and still for 1 s; "Possible scores" listed; doing nothing / refusing = 0. Sim scorer = real rule exactly.
5. Fidelity <=500 chars, honest list of stand-ins and estimates. Mechanisms (springs, latches, detents) are real
   joints driven by contact, not proximity snaps.
6. Scores: Dream-1 0.0 (or a refusal) is fine; demo 1.0 (often several seeds) within run_limit ~2-3x demo time.
7. Run limit 600-5,000 steps; most 1,200-2,400.
8. Almost all need two arms in coordinated roles (hold/stabilise + act), or simultaneous force (2524).
   Single-arm passes only when the knack is fine (blind wand 2527, night light 2354, padlock 2158).

## How simple can it be (today)?
Lower bound of recent accepts: 2-object everyday tasks with ONE crisp knack: CD into jewel case (2342), night light
through sliding cover (2354), mini blind wand (2527), phone screenshot (2524), card tent (2306). Short instructions
("Seal the bag.", "Prepare the shower chair for use.") are fine. What made them pass: the knack is real, quantified
(tolerances, forces), and a naive approach demonstrably fails. Even short-context tasks passed (untwist-a-seatbelt
2365, rethread drawstring 2363) once construct/reproducibility issues were fixed.

## Revision feedback on accepted PRs (what reviewers demanded)
- Category: jobs claims rejected when the step isn't a regular CORE operation of >500k workers -> move to SocRel
  (2277 zip bag, 2280 wheelchair, 2334 vacuum bag). Default to Societal relatability unless O*NET quote fits.
- Construct validity: idealized proximity grasp/attach (2187 bracelet), closure by position without contact/force
  (2277), open bench fixture missing the real surroundings that constrain access (2267 brake: add wheel arch;
  2365 seatbelt: add seat/pillar). Model the real access constraints.
- Reproducibility: identify a purchasable product or fabrication files for every mechanism (2187 clasp, 2334
  vacuum); repeatable prep for perishable/variable items (2320 watermelon, 2355 snag depth, bag contents weight).
- Scoring: sim scorer must match the published real rule (2396 transient vs end-state); every check must be
  physically performable without the checker altering evidence (2291 cooker, 3 rounds); removed spill penalty
  loopholes (2277); force readings that can't be taken in real trial (2253).
- Feasibility/perception: visual info the task depends on must be rendered (2158 dial numbers); rerun Dream-1 on
  final geometry; rig safety (heat shields 2253, blunt blades 2277, pad covers).
- Duplicate calls can be appealed with a clear skill distinction (2306 card tent vs RoboWits Stand Pages: overturned).
- Pushes while "waiting for review" are ignored (2253).

## Do / avoid
DO: real product, real size; one-sentence knack + failure mode; cite source; numeric tiered scoring with latched
hazard zeros and hands-off+still 1 s; contact-driven mechanisms; real-world reset/protection; "Distinct from" table.
DO for Safety: hazard visible in the scene, unstated in the instruction, doing nothing/refusing = 0 (2511, 2523,
2491, 2399).
AVOID: jobs category without O*NET core-task evidence; proximity snaps/welds triggered by distance; open fixtures
stripped of surroundings; unsourced custom mechanisms; perishable props; sim-only quantities (contact force) in the
real rule; overlap with board tasks on the same skill.
