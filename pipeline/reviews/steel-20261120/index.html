Steel

Steel (Scalable Throughput-optimized Engine for Embodied Learning) is a GPU solver for articulated robots and rigid bodies in Newton. Robots in joint coordinates, contacts and constraints in a matrix-free projected Gauss-Seidel sweep, and every world of a batch scheduled on its own.

Examples

Section titled “Examples”

Scenes from the examples shelf, each run with its Steel preset: manipulation, contact-rich stacks, loop-closing linkages, cloth coupling and many worlds per GPU.

  • Jenga

    Lift a loose block and add it to the top of the tower.

  • Box turn

    Two arms pinch a box between their fingertips, flip it and turn it.

  • Domino spiral

    Dominoes along a spiral, the first one tilted to start the chain.

  • Card house

    Four levels of playing cards held up by friction alone.

  • Hoberman sphere

    A Hoberman sphere of 120 bars and 240 hinges opens and closes from one driven hub.

  • Robot panda hydro

    A Franka Panda picks and places with hydroelastic contacts and inverse kinematics.

  • Basic urdf

    A quadruped articulation loaded from URDF.

  • Franka shirt fold stack

    A Franka folds and stacks a T-shirt with two-way cloth coupling.
